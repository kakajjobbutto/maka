1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Kyenkanyi (the “App”), developed by KAKAJJOBBUTTO (the “Developer”), handles information when you use the App. Kyenkanyi is an educational game in which players match equivalent fractions across progressively challenging levels.

The App is intended for individuals aged 13 and above. According to the App's stated design, it works offline and does not require a user account, login, or submission of personal information.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or other identifying information to play. We do not use the App to track users or build advertising profiles.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in that email solely so we can respond to your enquiry. This email correspondence takes place outside the App's gameplay.

3. Game progress stored on your device

To make the game useful across sessions, Kyenkanyi saves certain gameplay information locally on your device, such as unlocked levels, stars earned, and best score. This information is used to restore your progress and is not transmitted to us through the App.

Local gameplay records are not a user account. Removing the App or clearing its storage may erase your saved progress. Device-level backup features, if enabled by your operating system, are managed by the device/platform rather than by a Kyenkanyi account.

4. Offline operation

Kyenkanyi's gameplay works without an internet connection. The App does not require online registration, cloud synchronization, or a remote gameplay service.

5. Device permissions

Kyenkanyi is designed not to request access to sensitive device capabilities such as your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or files. The App does not require users to grant permissions in order to play.

6. Advertising, analytics, and third parties

The App is described as having no advertising SDKs, analytics tracking, or third-party services that collect user information. We do not sell or share personal data collected through the App, because the App does not collect such data.

Distribution platforms such as Google Play may independently process information relating to downloads, installation, or purchases under their own privacy policies. Such platform processing is separate from the App's own information practices.

7. Age suitability and children's privacy

Kyenkanyi is intended for individuals 13 years of age and older. It is not specifically directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from users of any age.

If you have a question about age suitability or privacy, please contact us using the address below.

8. Data security

Because Kyenkanyi does not transmit gameplay data to developer-operated servers, its design limits exposure of user information. Locally saved game progress remains subject to the security and backup settings of your device. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your choices and data deletion

You can play without creating an account or providing personal information. You can remove locally saved progress by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App, subject to any operating-system backup behavior. There is no Kyenkanyi cloud account to delete.

For questions about any email correspondence you have sent to us, contact the developer directly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features or privacy practices change, or to meet applicable legal requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes to information practices will be reflected in the policy before the changed features are made available.
